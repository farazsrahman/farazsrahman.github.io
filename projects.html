Model-Based Learning for Locomotion

I am currently working on an extension of the DIAL-MPC paper which uses sampling-based optimization and a lot of compute to perform legged locomotion tasks training-free. Our team is interested in incorporating learning into the algorithm to reduce its computational demands. The goal is to test sim2real on the Unitree Go2 quadruped platform.

Layer-wise Preconditioners & Provable Feature Learning

Recent excitement around layer-wise preconditioners (e.g. Shampoo, SOAP) had grown due to strong performance in online competitions such as AlgoPerf. We pose the question of *why* these methods perform well and answer them by studying linear representation learning which naive SGD catastrophically fails on. This project was lead by Thomas Zhang and featured in ICML 2025.

Decision Making Diffusion Models

Thinking of DDPMS as a stochastic process, we explore how different time intervals in the diffusion trajectory affect the features structure that shows up in the final sample. More importantly, I learned to train large models on (multiple) GPUs, got my toes wet with generative modeling theory, and made a lot of cool friends while working on this project in Prof. Eric Wong's Brachio Lab under Anton Xue during my Sophomore summer.

TyPorch

TyPorch began as means for me to learn how ML works beyond model.fit() after my freshman Big Data course, but unintentionally became my first interaction with optimization algorithms, high performance (parallel) computing, C++, and shaped many of my interests throughout the rest of my time at Penn. I was able to train moderately sized (10k parameter) feedforward models on toy data, but never finished working all the way through benchmarking on the MNIST dataset.
